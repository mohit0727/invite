Our Story

How it all began

Funnily enough, we sat in the same classroom for years — passing each other in the corridors, sharing the same teachers, never once exchanging a word. Life had quietly placed us side by side, waiting for the right moment.

That moment came years later, through our parents. What began as a formal introduction quickly turned into easy conversation, then long ones, and then the kind that make you forget to check the time. Within a week, we both knew.

Sometimes the person you've been looking for has been right there all along — you just needed someone to make the introduction. We'd be honoured to have you with us as we say "I do."
